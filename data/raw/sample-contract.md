# Master Services Agreement

## Term and Renewal

The agreement begins on January 1, 2026 and continues for two years. It renews for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice sixty days before expiration.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured for thirty days after written notice. The customer may terminate any statement of work for convenience with ninety days notice.

## Confidentiality

Each party must protect confidential information using reasonable care and may disclose it only to employees, contractors, or advisors who need access to perform under the agreement.

## Intellectual Property

Pre-existing intellectual property remains owned by the original owner. Deliverables created specifically for the customer are assigned to the customer after full payment, except for provider tools and reusable know-how.
